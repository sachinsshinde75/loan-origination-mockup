Why two prototypes?

Different Audiences

LoanFlow answers "what does a modern lending journey look like end-to-end?" — useful for ops leaders, sales teams, and business partners understanding the full scope. Praman answers "where does AI actually add unique value?" — useful for investors, technical hires, and NBFCs evaluating our underwriting engine as a standalone tool.

Honest Architecture

Not everything needs to be an "AI agent" — most of a lending system is deterministic software (BRE rules, API calls, workflow state machines). Agents matter only where LLM judgment adds real value: parsing unstructured documents, explaining variance, writing narrative. Both prototypes label services and agents clearly, no marketing overreach.

One Platform Underneath

Under the hood, LoanFlow and Praman share the same modular monolith backend, the same database, the same design language. Praman can run standalone as a B2B tool sold to other NBFCs, or embed inside LoanFlow's Step 9 (Bank Statement analysis). Same code, two surfaces.
