📄 AGENT WRITTEN Executive Summary · CAM Narrative Agent

Sharma Electronics presents as a stable retail trading business with 12 months of consistent cashflow through their HDFC Bank current account. Observed monthly credits average ₹22.85L, which reconciles closely with the ₹23L declared revenue (99.3% match). Debits average ₹21.10L, driven primarily by vendor payments to consumer electronics distributors — a pattern consistent with the declared retail-trading nature of the business.

Two variance flags surfaced during reconciliation, both of which appear explainable rather than adverse. First, ITR-declared income of ₹18.5L annually appears low relative to declared monthly profit of ₹3.5L (₹42L annualised), but this is consistent with a cash-heavy retail business scaling up in FY25 — GST returns confirm the higher current turnover. Second, one outward cheque bounce in July 2025 for ₹28k appears isolated with no subsequent pattern; balance data confirms adequate funds were available at the time, suggesting a technical or timing issue rather than liquidity stress.

Cashflow stability and health indicators are strong. Minimum end-of-day balance across 12 months was ₹1.24L (no month with sub-lakh EoD), no inward bounces or ECS returns, and the account displays typical B2B patterns rather than salary-like credits — appropriate for the declared applicant profile. FOIR post-new-loan is projected at 24.5% including existing obligations, well within the 55% policy cap.

Recommendation: Approve at requested ₹15L, 36-month tenure, at rate band 14.75-16.25% p.a. depending on final documentation quality. Consider PDC + PG as standard conditions given the ITR variance flag, though the underlying business narrative is sound.
